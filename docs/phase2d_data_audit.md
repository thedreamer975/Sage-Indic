# Phase 2D Data and Evidence Source Audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-02  
**Status:** Source audit and design only. No source is approved as the Phase 2D factual evidence corpus. No data was downloaded.

## Scope and decision

Phase 2D needs (1) evidence documents whose source and reuse terms are documented and (2) a separate retrieval evaluation set with independently established relevance. The repository currently contains neither. Existing tokenizer/safety data and external language datasets are not automatically factual evidence.

Status terms used below:

- **Verified metadata:** directly observed in an identified author/maintainer dataset card, repository, or treebank page on the review date.
- **Unresolved:** authoritative records conflict or do not establish intended use/underlying-content rights. Do not use until resolved.
- **Excluded:** not suitable as factual evidence for the stated role, even if potentially useful for another research purpose.

This is not legal advice and does not determine permissions beyond what the named source states.

### Source classes considered but not approved

No specific authoritative Telugu public-domain/open-license factual-document source was verified and approved in this audit. Government, cultural-institution, and scholarly publishers are source *types* to investigate, not citations or approved sources. A source must be selected by a human and its exact content license, publisher, language/script, publication/update date, stable URL/identifier, and reuse terms checked before acquisition.

### IndicQA discrepancy resolution

The current HF page for the AI4Bharat dataset displays **CC-BY-4.0**. The AI4Bharat IndicBERT repository describes IndicQA as a manually curated cloze-style QA dataset and says datasets created as part of the work are **CC-0**. Those records do not agree on the license shown for the concrete hosted dataset. The local `docs/data_manifest.md` repeats CC-0, but a local statement cannot settle the conflict.

Therefore:

1. **Current stated license:** conflicting primary records: HF card CC-BY-4.0; author repository CC-0.
2. **Is local statement outdated?** Cannot determine. It is consistent with the author repository statement but conflicts with the current dataset-host listing.
3. **Can it be used?** This audit does not make a legal conclusion. **Unresolved; do not use** until AI4Bharat or another authoritative rights record resolves the concrete release and intended research use.
4. **Attribution:** If CC-BY-4.0 governs, its terms require attribution and specified notices, license link, and indication of changes when sharing; see [Creative Commons BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If CC0 governs, it is a different basis. Do not select one condition absent resolution. Scholarly citation remains appropriate regardless.
5. **Derived artifact redistribution:** unresolved, including whether query annotations, processed contexts, or evidence extracts may be redistributed under the stated dataset terms and source-text rights.
6. **Use decision:** do not use IndicQA directly for the corpus, retrieval evaluation, or derived annotations at this time.

### Exclusions and limitations

- Translation, QA, tokenizer, and linguistic resources are not factual evidence corpora merely because they contain Telugu text.
- Corpus truth/authority must come from the actual publisher/source and human source review, not a retrieval score or dataset card label.
- License statements about a dataset packaging or annotations do not automatically resolve rights in underlying text.
- The sources above do not supply the project’s missing, human-verified relevance judgments across all three query modalities.
- No benchmark query or evidence record has been created as part of this audit.
